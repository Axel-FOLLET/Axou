01

Context engineering: Claude and Codex

Three goals, in this order: accurate data, controlled freedom for the agents, optimised use.

  • Shared rules

    One source for both assistants: a core read in every conversation (accuracy, communication, permissions), then eleven modules, from guidance to security, loaded when their topic comes up.

  • Levels of evidence

    Every statement declares its level: observed, reported by me, hypothesis or proposal. A figure cites its source; a report separates what was observed from what was inferred.

  • Skills

    Six methods shared by Claude and Codex: explaining code, finding the reason behind a choice, designing before writing, reviewing a change, guiding a regression test, teaching at my pace. Alongside them, rule modules such as writing: nine rules and my own style, drawn from my master's thesis.

  • Hooks

    Automatic checks at each step: rules recalled with every message, file writes refused without a recognised approval, then invisible characters and image metadata flagged. Automated tests check them.

  • Multi-agent team

    For delegated work, four separate roles: the manager writes the mission brief with me, the project lead splits and checks the work, agents carry it out, and an expert reviews the result. Without a “compliant” verdict, nothing is delivered.

  • Learning log

    A written record of each session, quizzes marked on content and spaced reviews: difficulties come back sooner, mastered topics less often.
