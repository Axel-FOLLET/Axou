## Inspirations

Nothing is copied from these works, and no image, logo, sound or model was taken from them: *Stray* (cat behaviour, studied from public videos), *Tiny Glade* (meadow palette and stone), *Code Lyoko* (construction of the arena), *Dark Souls* and *Elden Ring* (health bars, fight atmosphere and boss music), the invincibility star of *Mario* (star Chagiciens, planned), the *Star Wars* opening crawl (credits, planned).
